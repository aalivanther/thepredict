Getting started

ThePredict docs

ThePredict gives your MythicMobs mobs and bosses a predictive combat brain. You keep writing the skills; ThePredict decides when and how each one comes out.

ThePredict (tP) is an addon for MythicMobs Premium 5.13.1 on Paper 26.2 with Java 25. Bind a mob to a short profile and it stops spamming skills on timers: it reads the fight every couple of ticks, keeps its spacing, backs off, flanks a raised shield, punishes a target that drinks, and fires each skill the moment it makes sense.

Info Release candidate

ThePredict 0.0.1 is a release candidate. These pages describe what this build does, checked against its source code, but it has not been verified on a live Paper server yet. A key marked no effect yet is accepted but not used by this build. If your server behaves differently from what you read here, that is a bug worth reporting (see Troubleshooting & status).

#Who does what

ThePredict does not replace MythicMobs. It sits on top of it and only takes the decisions.

WhoWhat it does
MythicMobsDefines the mob, its skills, conditions, targeters, cooldowns, effects and projectiles. Picks the target with its threat table. Moves the mob with its goals and the native navigator.
ThePredictGenerates the mob's movement goals from the profile (they replace its AIGoalSelectors), picks the tactic, publishes the current intent through predictor* conditions, placeholders and signals, fires the skills at the right tick and adds threat for things MythicMobs does not measure.
Jev (TypeSafe's decision model)Picks among the tactics that are valid right now and returns probabilities. It never executes anything. Optional: without a key, ThePredict decides locally, at no cost.

#How it decides

A deterministic engine does the heavy work every tick; Jev only makes the final pick among a few candidates it has prepared.

1 · ReadEvery 2 ticksTarget, distance band, health bands, shields, drawn bows, cooldowns, terrain.
2 · PrepareCandidate tacticsContexts filter, reach and readiness checks, duplicates merged, each option described in plain English.
3 · AskOne request per fightEvery 20 ticks or sooner when something changes. Jev returns a probability per option, plus "what if" contingencies.
4 · ActWithin ticksFires ready skills on their own cooldown, reacts to openings, re-checks reach and conditions before every cast.

The mobs that fight the same players share one encounter and one request: a boss and its adds cost one request per heartbeat, with a question per mob. Requests never block the server.

#The fallback chain

If Jev is slow, unsure or unreachable, the mob never freezes. It drops down a four-level chain and climbs back up on the next good answer.

LevelShown asWho decides
1JEVJev's answer.
2ALTERNATIVEThe next valid option, or a contingency, of that same answer.
3LOCALThe profile itself: active context and tactic priority, no network, no cost.
4BASEThe mob's base AI (its BaseAI or AIGoalSelectors). ThePredict does not control it.

/thepredict inspect nearest tells you the current level and the last reason for a drop. The reasons are listed in Troubleshooting.

#What it never does

  • No effects. ThePredict never adds particles, sounds, glow, animations or markers, and never edits your skills or MythicMobs files. Tells and stances are signals (~onSignal:PREDICTOR_*) and placeholders that your own skills can react to.
  • No hidden reads. It decides with what the mob could perceive: participants in range, visible details only with line of sight. A player's movement is read from what they actually do, never from raw keyboard input.
  • No names to Jev. Jev receives bands and facts in English with aliases (player_1, entity_1 plus its species), never names, UUIDs, coordinates, Display names or MythicMobs IDs.
  • No surprise costs. Without a key nothing leaves your server. With a key, every request fits the budgets you set in config.yml.
