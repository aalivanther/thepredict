Administration

Troubleshooting & status

Find out why a mob is not doing what you expect, from the console, /thepredict status and /thepredict inspect.

#First checks

  1. /thepredict status. Read State (the Jev service), Profiles (the catalog), Bound mobs (who ThePredict controls, by level) and Recent failures / Recent fallbacks.
  2. /thepredict inspect nearest next to the mob. Session says whether it is bound, Intent what it is doing, Why the last fallback and cast result, Goals whether its movement goals are installed.
  3. The console. Every profile problem names the file and the path: <file>: <profile>.<path>: <message>. For a live decision log, set decision.logDecisions to true and restart.
  4. Reload or restart? Profiles, mob and skill files: /mm reload. config.yml and the key: restart the server, there is no reload command. After /minecraft:reload (datapack tags), run /mm reload too.

#Service states

The State line of /thepredict status and the answer of /thepredict check.

ValueMeaningWhat to do
UNCONFIGUREDNo key is configured.Set the TYPESAFE_API_KEY environment variable (or jev.apiKey) and restart. Meanwhile mobs fight with their local planner, at no cost.
INVALID_CONFIGURATIONconfig.yml is invalid; the console names the file, the path and the reason.Fix the file and restart. ThePredict stays inactive; MythicMobs keeps working.
CHECKINGThe service is being checked (at startup, or after a pause).Wait for the result in the console or in /thepredict status.
READYThe service accepts requests.Nothing.
PAUSEDRequests are paused after a rate limit (429), an overload (529), network or service errors, two timeouts in a row, three invalid answers in a row, or a rejected request (60 s).Wait: ThePredict retries with a growing pause (1 to 60 s), then a single trial request. If it repeats, check roundtrip latency and the limits.
INVALID_CREDENTIALSThe key was rejected (401).Check the key in your TypeSafe account, set it and restart: the key is read only at startup.
INVALID_MODELThe configured model does not exist.Use the tested model ID jev-1.13.0 and restart.

With INVALID_CREDENTIALS (a 401 or 403) or INVALID_MODEL, ThePredict sends nothing until a restart or a /thepredict check; mobs fight locally meanwhile. The key itself is read only at startup.

#Fallback reasons

When a mob drops to a lower control level, the reason shows in inspect (Why: last fallback …), in status (fallbacks and Recent fallbacks) and, with decision.logDecisions, as Mob <uuid> fell back: <REASON>.

ValueMeaningWhat to do
SERVICE_PAUSEDThe service is paused (429, 529, timeout, network) or still being checked.Wait out the pause; check latency and limits if it repeats.
SERVICE_BLOCKEDThe service cannot be used without a configuration change: UNCONFIGURED, INVALID_CONFIGURATION, INVALID_CREDENTIALS or INVALID_MODEL.Read State in /thepredict status, fix the key or the model and restart.
NO_BUDGETThe request, token or in-flight budget is spent (server, world or encounter).Review decision.maxRequestsPerMinute, decision.maxInputTokensPerMinute and the load. Mobs without budget fight locally, without errors.
LOW_CONFIDENCESeveral answers in a row stayed below the minimum confidence.Review the profile: contexts, tactic names and descriptions.
INVALID_RESPONSEThe answer had no applicable tactic.Make sure the profile offers eligible tactics; report it with the log if it repeats.
EXPIREDThe intent expired without a replacement.Normal now and then. If frequent, compare roundtrip latency with profileDefaults.maximumIntentDurationTicks.
NO_PROGRESSThe movement made no progress for two decisions (target on a pillar, behind a wall, in water).Check the terrain, the arena and the mob's species.
RETREAT_LIMITThe encounter already has as many simultaneous retreats as it allows (one per three members, at least one).Nothing; it is coordination between mobs.
SESSION_INVALIDThe mob was reloaded, died or changed world.Nothing; it is transient.
INTERNAL_ERRORAn exception inside ThePredict; the console says how many sessions fell back to the base AI.Report it with the log. The mob returns to its base AI.

#Other counters

The Counters section of status groups running totals. Each group lists only non-zero values, or none. totals also counts options_merged (options not sent because they read like another option) and the contingencies asked, fired and effective.

ValueMeaning

MODEL_UNAVAILABLEThe model does not exist.

TIMEOUTNo answer arrived within jev.timeoutMillis.

#dispatch

What happened to an encounter that was due for a decision.

ValueMeaning
SENTA request was sent.
EMPTY_CAPTURENo member of the encounter could be read.
NO_QUESTIONSNo mob qualified for a question (an active session with installed goals, no debug intent, a perceived target and two distinct eligible tactics), and there is no group plan question.
SERVICE_UNAVAILABLEThe service is not ready, is paused, or is running its single trial request.
NO_BUDGETA request, token or in-flight limit refused the prepared request; the mobs play locally.
NOTHING_TO_ASKWhile preparing, no question kept two options that read differently: nothing is sent or reserved and the mobs play locally.
PREPARE_FAILEDThe request could not be encoded (for example, over the size guard). It counts as INVALID_REQUEST, nothing is sent, that encounter plays locally and the service is not paused, so other encounters keep asking.
CLIENT_ERRORThe client failed to accept the request (a bug); counted as INVALID_REQUEST.
CACHE_HITAn identical earlier answer was reused (only with decision.answerCache on).

#outcomes

Result of trying to cast the skill of an intent.

ValueMeaning
CASTThe skill was cast.
DEFERRED_INVULNERABLEThe target is in its invulnerability frames; the cast is retried in a later tick.
SKIPPED_SHIELDThe target blocks with a shield and the skill cannot ignore it.
REJECTED_BY_MYTHICMOBSMythicMobs refused the skill (a failed chance, a cancelled event, no charges left).
NOT_ELIGIBLEThe skill is not ready yet (cooldown, reach, conditions); retried in a later tick.
EXPIREDThe intent expired before the skill could be cast.

Why an answer that arrived was not applied.

ValueMeaning
STALE_SEQUENCEA newer request of the same encounter was already sent; the older answer is dropped.
SESSION_CHANGEDThe mob's session changed while the request was in flight (it was reloaded, respawned or moved to another encounter).
SESSION_INACTIVEThe mob's session is not active, or a debug intent holds it.
CATALOG_CHANGEDProfiles or MythicMobs were reloaded while the request was in flight.
MODEL_CHANGEDA model other than the configured one answered.
TOO_OLDThe answer arrived after it could still be applied (see decision.maxDecisionAgeMillis).
TARGET_CHANGEDThe mob changed target; the answer was about the old one.
PROBE_UNAVAILABLEThe mob could not be read when the answer arrived.
SITUATION_CHANGEDA reactive answer no longer matched the live situation.
GROUP_EPOCH_CHANGEDThe group plan was captured under another group membership; the leader's question is kept.

Changing soon Tell warnings

Warnings about tells (for example telegraph has no visual (or audio) cue; ThePredict adds none: add a ~onSignal:PREDICTOR_TELL_<ANSWER> handler) belong to the tell system, which will be redefined soon. ThePredict never adds effects: the warning asks you to react to the signal in your own skill.

#Effectiveness

Each time a mob adopts a new intent at level JEV, ALTERNATIVE or LOCAL (never on a renewal), ThePredict opens an outcome window. It closes when the intent ends or after 60 ticks, whichever comes first, and records:

  • damage dealt and taken, as fractions of the victim's max health;
  • casts, and confirmed hits (the target took damage within 20 ticks of a damaging cast);
  • whether the movement reached its goal (APPROACH got to melee, KEEP_DISTANCE ended at range, RETREAT ended farther than it started, FLANK cast while flanking; HOLD and NONE have no goal);
  • whether the mob or its target died.
WhereWhat to read
status, Effectiveness: outcomes: level=JEV windows=3 dealt=0.12 taken=0.03 reach=0.67 hit_rate=0.50 effect=0.21One line per level. dealt and taken are means per window, reach the share of windows that reached their goal, hit_rate confirmed hits per cast, effect the mean of dealt − taken + 0.25·reached + target died − mob died. Without data: outcomes: no closed window yet.
status: contingencies: asked=4 fired=2 effective=1Contingencies sent, fired in combat, and fired with a positive window.
inspect: Outcomes: JEV keep_distance_and_shoot dealt=0.12 taken=0.03 reached=yes casts=1 hits=1; …The mob's last 8 windows, newest last, with mob died or target died when it happened.
Console with decision.logDecisions: Outcome JEV keep_distance_and_shoot dealt=0.12 …One line per closed window.

The effect index is a comparison tool, not a verdict: use it to compare levels or two versions of the same profile.

#Common symptoms

The mob ignores ThePredict. Check, in order: Predictor: true in the mob file; a profile whose MythicID matches the mob ID exactly (case-sensitive); no profile error in the console; inspect says Goals: installed for revision <n>. Species with a Brain (piglins, wardens, villagers…) only accept Movement: NONE, and slimes, magma cubes, ghasts, phantoms and bats keep their own AI (Goals: not installed (MythicMobs keeps its AI)).

It fights, but always at LOCAL. Normal without a key. With a key, read State in status and the Why line of inspect: SERVICE_BLOCKED, NO_BUDGET and LOW_CONFIDENCE are the usual causes.

A skill spams. With the default CastMode: any_ready, every skill with a valid Intention line fires as soon as it is ready. A skill without a native Cooldown is ready all the time. Give it a Cooldown (or Charges); ThePredict adds no floor of its own.

Double damage. The mob's own trigger lines (~onAttack, ~onTimer) keep running while ThePredict controls it. Gate them with predictorcontrol false so they only fire when ThePredict is not in control.

The boss hits less than its classic version. While ThePredict controls a mob, its base melee goal does not hit. Put the melee damage in a skill of a tactic, or add a Kind: VANILLA_MELEE choice (one vanilla hit every 20 ticks at most).

A predictor* condition is always false. The control conditions (predictorcontrol, predictormove, predictortactic, predictorrole, predictorplan, predictortoken, predictorzone, predictorpattern) are false at BASE and once the intent expires; predictorstance is the exception. predictorzone only has data with an effective zone mode of full, and predictorpattern only in the bias or full pattern modes. A wrong argument makes the condition always false and logs a warning at load.

A signal never fires. The handler must be a line ~onSignal:PREDICTOR_<NAME> (or a bare ~onSignal) in the Skills of the mob or of its Template. A handler written inside another skill does not count, because MythicMobs does not register it either. Write the signal name in upper case. PREDICTOR_PATTERN_* signals are only sent in the bias and full pattern modes, and PREDICTOR_PLAN_* and PREDICTOR_ROLE_* only while the group has a plan (see Groups).

A signal fires again and again. A local mob that makes no progress drops to level 4 and picks the same tactic again at the next decision, and each return counts as a new tactic: its PREDICTOR_<TACTIC> signal can fire around 30 times a minute. Do not put effects that must not repeat in such a handler, and fix the terrain or the arena (NO_PROGRESS).

The decision is dropped when the target changes. When MythicMobs picks a new target, the decision in progress is discarded and the mob runs its base AI until the next capture of its encounter (2 to 4 ticks, around 22 at most).

Two groups that meet stay separate. Mobs that started fighting in two different places keep two encounters and two requests, even after they join.

#Decision log

With decision.logDecisions set to true (and a restart), the console prints every decision, fallback, cast result, threat change and closed outcome window, at most 20 lines every 10 seconds. No player names, only aliases:

Console
Mob <uuid> fell back: NO_PROGRESS
Threat +1.50 ALLY_ASSISTED for player_2 sent to the table of mob <uuid>
Threat -2.00 UNREACHABLE for player_1 sent to the table of mob <uuid>
Outcome JEV keep_distance_and_shoot dealt=0.12 taken=0.03 reached=yes casts=1 hits=1

Local output may show the full UUID of the mob; player names never appear.

When you report a bug, include the status and inspect output, these console lines, the profile and the mob and skill files, and your versions. Never include the key: ThePredict never prints it, so nothing you copy from its output contains it.
